Everything you check daily, together

Stop bouncing between four logins. UniCombined pulls it all into one calm, fast app.

Today view

Every deadline from myCourses, Crowdmark and Ed in one list, sorted by what's due next.

Weekly schedule

Your classes laid out week by week, with rooms and times, straight from Minerva.

Ed threads, unread counts

See which courses have new Ed activity at a glance, and read threads without leaving the app.

Courses, grades and GPA

Course cards with your latest grades, plus your unofficial Minerva transcript and GPA.

Add/drop by CRN

Register or drop a section by CRN in seconds, no Minerva menu maze.

Instant notifications

A push the moment a new assignment is posted or a new grade drops, on any of the four.

Your data stays on your phone

UniCombined has no middleman server. The app talks directly to each service, the same way your browser does.

  • Sign-ins and session tokens are stored only on your device.
  • Your grades, transcript and messages are never uploaded to us.
  • This waitlist only stores your email address, and only to tell you when the app launches.
